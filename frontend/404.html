About Finora

About Finora

Finora is a personal finance management application designed to help you track income, manage expenses, monitor budgets, and build better saving habits. Our goal is to make managing your money simpler, clearer, and more organized.

Track Your Money

Manage income and expenses in one place. Keep an accurate, real-time log of every transaction.

Plan Your Budget

Monitor spending and stay within your limits. Receive clear thresholds before overspending.

Grow Your Savings

Set financial goals and follow your progress. Build financial resilience step by step.
